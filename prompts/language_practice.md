# Practising a language in chat

Use when the user says "let's practise Japanese", "quiz me in Telugu", or answers a weekly language question in chat.

1. Read `config/languages.json` for the language, level and goal. Optionally look at the last few packs or lessons (`content/language/`, the latest weekly package) to practise what they've seen.
2. Keep it conversational and short-turned:
   - **Quiz:** ask one question at a time (translate, respond, choose the polite form). Wait for the answer, then correct gently: say what was right, show the natural version with script and romanization, and explain in one line.
   - **Role-play:** set a scene from recent themes (café, station, meeting someone). Play the other person, keep sentences at their level, and add romanization and meaning in brackets when useful.
   - **Speaking:** give a line to say aloud, then ask them to type what they said or how it felt; offer pronunciation tips (pitch, long vowels, retroflex sounds) in plain words.
3. End with 2–3 phrases worth remembering.
4. Nothing from practice is saved, except: if they ask to change level, goals or focus, update `config/languages.json`; if they say "more of this", record it as feedback (`more_of`).
